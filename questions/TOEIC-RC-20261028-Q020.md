MUSEUM NOTICE: Tickets for the gallery tour may be refunded if a request and receipt are submitted to the ticket office within 14 days of purchase. Requests received after 14 days are not eligible. Exchanges are available only before the tour date.

What should a visitor do to obtain a refund?

1. Submit a receipt to the ticket office within 14 days.
2. Call the museum before visiting.
3. Exchange the ticket at the entrance on the event date.
4. Wait until the next business day.
